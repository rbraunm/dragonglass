DRAGONGLASS BENCHMARK COMPARISON — BEFORE vs AFTER
================================================================
Date:      May 17, 2026
Upgrade:   CPU swap + DIMM redistribution + pin surgery

BEFORE: 2x E5-2680 v3 (24c/48t), 330GB DDR4-1866, 8ch, 3 DPC
AFTER:  2x E5-2699 v4 (44c/88t), 320GB DDR4-2400, 8ch, 2 DPC
        NUMA: 148/198 GB (asymmetric) → 157/157 GB (symmetric)

NOTE: Slot A2 required pin surgery. Multiple cantilever pins in the
bottom-right quadrant of the LGA 2011-3 socket (DDR Channel 1 data
pins, rows 1-11 columns BT-DF) were depressed or misaligned. Repair
performed with a dental tool under phone-camera magnification.

================================================================
VM CONTENTION IMPACT
================================================================
With the larger L3 cache (55MB vs 30MB), VM contention has a bigger
impact than before. The 7b model benefits most from cache when VMs
are off, suggesting its hot weights fit in the expanded cache:

Model                    VMs-on    VMs-off   Impact
qwen2.5-coder:7b          4.6       5.6       +22%
deepseek-r1:14b            2.6       3.0       +15%
qwen2.5-coder:14b          3.1       3.0        ~0%

================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================
1. CPU upgrade (cache + IPC + clock) provides ~35% generation speed
   improvement across all models under production conditions.

2. The 55MB L3 cache is the biggest single contributor — it reduces
   memory round trips during token generation, which is the bottleneck
   for CPU-only inference.

3. DDR4-2400 at 2 DPC delivers 87 GB/s actual bandwidth (vs theoretical
   153.6 GB/s peak), a massive improvement over the old 3-DPC config.

4. NUMA symmetry (157/157 vs 148/198) eliminates cross-socket penalty
   for workloads that span both nodes.

5. VM contention now matters more due to L3 cache competition. This
   strengthens the case for GPU offload (Phase 5/6) where inference
   has dedicated VRAM with zero host contention.
